GRSJ HRMS v1.0.5 - FINAL ATTENDANCE RULES

1) Sunday salary-day rule
- Every Sunday is automatically credited as Present.
- Sandwich exception: if BOTH Saturday and Monday are Absent, Sunday becomes Absent.
- Auto Sunday rows are recalculated safely as adjacent attendance changes.
- Real/manual Sunday punches are never overwritten.
- This supports full-month salary including Sundays.

2) Leave + Absent merged
- Attendance status "Leave" is retired from the attendance register.
- Legacy attendance Leave rows are converted to Absent.
- XLSX "On Leave" imports as Absent.
- Approved full-day leave creates/uses Absent attendance.
- Approved half-day leave uses Half Day.
- Sunday inside a leave period still follows the Sat+Mon Sunday rule.
- Reports show Absent instead of Leave.

Leave Request screens remain as the HR approval workflow; only the attendance/payroll status is merged into Absent.
