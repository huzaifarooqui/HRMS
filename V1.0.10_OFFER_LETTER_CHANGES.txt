GRSJ HRMS v1.0.10 - Offer Letter Revision

1. Reporting To is now a dedicated individual senior-name field in the admin Offer Letter form.
2. Previous simple compensation table replaced with Annexure - I salary-components table:
   - Basic: 50% of monthly gross
   - HRA: balance after Basic and statutory bonus (48.5% under current structure)
   - Advance Monthly Statutory Bonus: 1.5%
   - Gross Salary
   - PF/ESIC applicability rows
   - CTC
   - Employee/Employer PF rows
   - Total Deduction
   - In Hand / Take Home
   - Monthly and Annual columns
3. Annexure - I is kept on its own page so the table never splits.
4. Golden office-address/contact footer removed from Offer Letter pages.
5. Work Location remains in the Offer Letter summary.
