5-6. Reports
- Report is transposed: metrics vertically on left, employees horizontally.
- Metrics: Total Days, Present, Late, Half Day, Leave, Final Days, Salary, Net Salary.
- Total Days = actual calendar days in selected month.
- Final Days late logic: combinations of 3 Late => 2 credited days, 2 Late => 1.5 credited days, 1 remaining Late => 1 credited day.
- Explicit Half Day = 0.5 credited day. Leave = 0 credited day. Holiday attendance status = 1 credited day.
- Net Salary = (Salary / Total Days) * Final Days.

7. Admin Documents

- Document Type -> Employee -> auto employee details -> date/reference/notes -> Generate.
- Generate currently records an Awaiting Template request only.
